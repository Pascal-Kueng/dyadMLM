# APIM decomposition: full-text verification and exclusions

Checked **6–7 October 2026**, using UZH institutional browser access and public
repositories. Companion to the [current review](literature-review.md) and the
[September citation audit](citation-audit-2026-09-13.md).

The focused question is whether a source explains or uses a partition of paired
outcome covariance into actor/partner predictor routes, grouped contributions,
or total explained nonindependence. Ordinary APIM coefficients, mediation
products, k ratios, outcome R², and fitted covariance matrices do not establish
that use. Substantial adjacent methods are retained for the manuscript's
estimation, level, covariate, and response-family context.

## Changes to the working list

Nine ordinary applications were screened in full and excluded: eight from
the nine-paper candidate queue, plus Jang et al. (2025) from the main list.
The already-identified repeat Burns (2020) abstract was also removed as a
separate entry. Vu et al. (2026), the remaining queue entry, was provisionally
excluded after abstract screening; it is not a full-text exclusion. This leaves
**51 main reference entries and no active candidates**, after adding Perry
et al. (2017) as adjacent visualization methodology. Five retained methods
sources have upgraded full-text annotations; Dwyer's calculation supplement
is now verified too. These are
reference counts, not counts of independent studies or
fully verified focal applications.

The six previously verified published four-route applications remain Dwyer,
Burns, Figueroa, Lee, Ferraris, and Fu. De Padova's grouped attribution and
Velten's total/covariate attribution also remain. This access audit found no
additional focal application; it does not establish exhaustive coverage or
priority for our proposed inference methods.

## Dwyer supplement: gap closed

The genuine [PMC appendix](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/instance/5512865/bin/NIHMS868627-supplement.pdf)
was retrieved after an automatic browser check. Both pages are verified.

## ILD level partition: additional verification

**ILD scope recheck, 7 October:** Gistelinck and Loeys (2019), reproduced in
the [author's dissertation](https://backoffice.biblio.ugent.be/download/8635510/8635511),
p. 102 and Table 3.5.2 (p. 104), explicitly allocate modeled same-day dependence
89% to daily residual covariance and 11% to random-intercept covariance.
The allocation uses remaining covariance after the predictor mean structure,
not actor/partner predictor-route contributions at either level. Both pages
and the covariance table were checked; the dissertation is saved locally.
This strengthens existing ILD level-partition prior art without adding a
reference or a focal decomposition application.

**Adjacent source added:** Perry et al. (2017),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/fam0000293), was already saved among the repository's
visualization references. Its main methods/results, figures and plotting code
were checked. It illustrates longitudinal actor/partner patterns with vector
fields; pp. 21–22 explicitly combine within-/between-couple coefficients.
It supplies no temporal levelwise covariance-route partition. The addition
brings the current inventory from 50 to 51, without adding a focal application.

## Remaining access limits

- **Vu et al. (2026):** Human Kinetics recognizes Zurich access but labels the
  article restricted; the user also could not access it. The
  [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42463118/) describes baseline
  SAATH data from 126 mother–daughter dyads, testing US/Asian acculturation
  associations with physical-activity variables. It reports ordinary APIM
  coefficients, standard errors and p values, with no indication of covariance
  decomposition or explained nonindependence. **Provisionally excluded from
  the active queue on 7 October, based on the abstract.** Full text remains
  unverified; absence of the focal method cannot be confirmed. This decision
  is separate from the full-text exclusion table above.
- **Kashy and Kenny (2000):** bounded author/publisher search did not locate the
  primary chapter text. Later editions are separate sources, not verification
  of this edition.
- **Kenny, Kashy and Cook (2006), printed APIM passage:** the official excerpt
  covers Chapter 1; the Chapter 7 website supplies companion materials, not
  the printed chapter. Its focal decomposition content remains unverified.
- **Kline (2016):** the publisher's old book link redirects to the fifth edition
  and its sample is dated 2023. No relevant fourth-edition full text was obtained.
  Dwyer's citation of the 2016 book remains verified; APIM-specific content does
  not follow from that citation.
- **Koch final version and 1998 completeness:** the verified preprint/supplement
  and relevant scanned sections do not close these narrower version/page gaps.

This was a targeted source audit, not a fresh exhaustive citation search.
Laws's publisher PDF/supplement now close its access gap; the separately
recorded NIH manuscript embargo until 7 April 2027 does not prevent this
full-text verification.
